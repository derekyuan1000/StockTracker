# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good intent. No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, discussions, or anywhere else connected to this project.

## What's expected

- Give and receive feedback constructively
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people
- Be patient with people who are learning
- Accept that others may approach problems differently

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment or intimidation of any kind
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing someone's private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Reporting

If something happens that violates this, email **derekyuan1000@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate these expectations.
